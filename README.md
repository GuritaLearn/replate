# Replate

## Overview
Replate connects everyday consumers, businesses/UMKM, and partner organizations (food banks, animal feed producers, compost facilities) to redistribute surplus food before it spoils.

## Key Features

*   **Flexible Transaction Modes**
    *   **Sale (Jual):** Buy discounted surplus food with partial quantity purchases.
    *   **Barter:** Exchange goods with an automatic freshness timer pause during negotiations.
    *   **Donation (Donasi):** Free claiming of surplus food for those in need.
*   **Smart Lifecycle & Categorization**
    *   Products categorized by condition: *Ready-to-eat*, *Perlu Diolah* (Needs Processing), or *Pakan/Kompos* (Feed/Compost).
    *   Automated timeout jobs (`products:process-timeout`) reroute near-expiry items to partners for composting or animal feed.
*   **Roles & Permissions**
    *   Multi-tier access for regular Users, Verified UMKM sellers, Mitra/Partners, and Admins.
*   **Gamification & Rewards**
    *   **RePoin:** A reward system incentivizing users who save and donate food.
*   **Safety & Disputes**
    *   Built-in dispute system for handling expired or spoiled items upon delivery.
